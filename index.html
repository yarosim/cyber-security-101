FAQ

Questions, answered

What format are the kits?

PDF (US Letter) inside a ZIP file. Worksheets are fillable — type into them in Adobe Acrobat Reader, Apple Preview, or a modern browser — or print and write by hand. The Small Business kit also includes editable Word (.docx) and Markdown templates.

How do I get my download?

Stripe redirects you to a private download page. Gumroad delivers the ZIP from your Gumroad receipt. Bookmark the page or save the ZIP right away. Lost it? Email pnsgloballlc@gmail.com with your receipt and we’ll resend it.

Do I need technical skills?

No. Everything is written for beginners: plain language, checklists, and links to each company’s official help page when menus differ on your device.

Which kit should I start with?

Worried about scam texts or protecting parents? Start with the Scam Text & Phishing Defense Kit. Want to secure your own accounts? The MFA & Passkey Pack. Run or help run a business? The Small Business kit. The bundle includes all three with a roadmap.

Where does the information come from?

Official guidance from the FTC, CISA, NIST (including SP 800-63B-4), and FBI/IC3, plus Google, Apple, and Microsoft help pages. Each kit ends with a dated sources page. We don’t use invented statistics.

Is this legal or compliance advice?

No. The kits are educational. The Small Business policies are starting templates — adapt them to your business and have a qualified advisor review anything you rely on for legal, regulatory, or contractual purposes.

Can I share the files?

Print as many copies as you need for your household (or, for the Small Business kit, within your own organization). Please don’t resell or post the files publicly.

Is checkout secure?

Payments are handled by Stripe or Gumroad on their own checkout pages. We never see or store your card number.

What’s your refund policy?

If a kit isn’t right for you, email pnsgloballlc@gmail.com within 14 days of purchase for a refund.
