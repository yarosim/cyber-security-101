Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-09-24. Draft — review before launch.

This store is a static website. We keep it simple and collect as little as possible.

What we collect

  • This website: no accounts, no cookies, no analytics or ad trackers, and no forms. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses for security and operations under the GitHub Privacy Statement.
  • Payments: purchases are processed by Stripe or Gumroad on their checkout pages. They collect your payment details and email to complete the purchase and send your receipt. We receive order information (such as name, email, product, and amount) but never your full card number. See the Stripe Privacy Policy and the Gumroad Privacy Policy.
  • Email: if you email us, we use your message and address only to respond (e.g., support, orders, refunds).

How we use it

To deliver your purchase, provide support, process refunds, and meet legal and tax obligations. We don’t sell or rent personal information, and we don’t send marketing emails unless you ask to be added to a list.

Third-party links

Links to YouTube, government sites, and vendor help pages are governed by those sites’ own policies. Download pages ask browsers not to send the page address to other sites.

Retention

Order and support records are kept as long as needed for the purposes above and for legal, tax, and accounting requirements.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you by emailing pnsgloballlc@gmail.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law.

Children

This store is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.
